FINANCIAL ENGINEERING

Trade economics beyond the visible spread

The financial layer aims to turn a visible price difference into an evidence-aware assessment of costs, size, required inventory and uncertainty.

GROSS ≠ NETOBSERVED ≠ REALIZED

Why is Gross Edge insufficient?

The initial buy/sell spread is only a signal. The economic result depends on fees on each leg, the asset used to pay fees, actual order-book depth, quantity rounding, legal minimum size and the ability to fund each venue independently.

Decision gate

Market depth and assessable size

The best displayed price does not represent all available volume. In a real order book, price and quantity vary across levels; the size of an opportunity and its economic result can change as trade size increases.

  • Assess cost against available depth, not just the best quote.
  • Respect each market's minimum size and precision rules.
  • Separate theoretical volume from volume supported by current evidence.
  • Preserve uncertainty when depth or order rules are incomplete.

Capital in cross-exchange arbitrage

Funds held at one exchange are not automatically spendable at another. A multi-market route must account for the required inventory of each asset at the correct venue. Therefore, allocating new capital and analyzing existing inventory are different problems.

New capital

An allocation scenario based on a stated budget and valuation; it separates manual funding needs from trade-supported actions.

Current inventory

Analysis of existing assets, account-level constraints and the opportunity cost of changing inventory composition.

Three financial concepts that must stay separate

ConceptWhat it representsWhat it is not
Arbitrage PnLEconomic result of the trade cycleTotal portfolio value movement
Portfolio Mark-to-MarketChange in value of held assetsCertain arbitrage profit
External Cash FlowDeposits, withdrawals or non-trade changesBot operating income

Without a valid, time-stamped valuation source, aggregating profits across assets can produce a misleading number. Reports should preserve native units or mark the valuation as unresolved.

Shadow: analysis without a fill claim

Shadow supports analysis of observed opportunities, recurring episodes, data coverage and capital scenarios. But visible order-book volume does not guarantee an order fill. Shadow output must remain distinct from simulated Paper activity and real realized profit.

Financial principle

If fees, inventory, depth or valuation are not sufficiently evidenced, the correct result may be NO_ACTION or INCONCLUSIVE—not an estimated profit.
