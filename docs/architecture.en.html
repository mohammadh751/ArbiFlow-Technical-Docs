Why separate responsibilities?

Market data

Provides market evidence; it does not prove account inventory or a fill.

Financial assessment

Can reject an opportunity on cost or depth grounds; it does not authorize an order.

Shadow analytics

Analyzes observations and scenarios; it is not the real financial ledger or fill authority.

Execution decision

Depends on fresh evidence, account constraints and explicit authorization; a Candidate is not enough.

Market Data is not Market Truth

Receiving a WebSocket message or a snapshot does not establish that an entire order book is valid. Source identity, timing, consistency across both sides, recovery state and coverage all matter.

Engineering rule

Incomplete data must not become a complete opportunity. If evidence is insufficient, the result remains inconclusive or non-actionable.

Authority boundary

Public observation and evidence analysis are separate from order-execution authority. Automated withdrawals or transfers between exchanges are outside the current scope. Paper/Real remain locked pending independent qualification.
