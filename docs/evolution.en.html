ArbiFlow evolution: from market scanning to decision engineering

Earlier releases were not simply discarded. Their lessons helped establish clearer boundaries between data, trade economics, capital and execution authority.

VERSION HISTORYENGINEERING DECISIONS

Generation 0.2.79 — a Non-Live operating environment

This generation moved beyond spread detection toward a unified runtime, shared market data, Shadow analysis, portfolio scenarios and an operator dashboard.

  • Market acquisition: public market feeds and targeted recovery, separating transport health from per-market validity.
  • Opportunity lifecycle: separating a price observation, an opportunity episode and a simulated execution cycle.
  • Shadow & portfolio: opportunity analytics and capital scenarios without general real-order authority.
  • Operator surface: status, data coverage, fee views and analytical read models.
Architectural outcome: a scanner evolved into a system with explicit state, evidence and authority boundaries.

Generation V3 — Shadow consolidation and field qualification

V3 consolidated scanning, Decimal-based economics, scenario capital, evidence capture and route-quality analysis. The important step was distinguishing offline results from field evidence.

R5.68 — a rewrite centered on Market Truth and evidence

The new rewrite was not primarily about adding more features. It focused on making the origin and validity of each data point explicit, identifying ownership of state, and determining what may legitimately progress to the next stage.

  • Separating public market data from private account evidence.
  • Recording provenance and state changes for candidates and important events.
  • Separating observed Gross Edge from verified Net economics and action authority.
  • Keeping the system conservative when data or evidence is incomplete.
  • Maintaining Paper/Real locks until independent qualification is complete.
Status: R5.68 is presented as a generation under development and qualification—not as a proven Live or profitable product.
