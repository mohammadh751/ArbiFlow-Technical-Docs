Examples of observed output—with a clear boundary between observation and profit

Rather than emphasizing a short test duration, this page presents examples of the evidence recorded during development. Each example is tied to its version and evidence level.

Example 1: one-hour V3 Shadow run

The V3 field report dated 10 September 2026 records a one-hour Shadow run. Its purpose was to examine market-data behavior and opportunity analytics—not to execute real trades.

What do these examples not prove?

  • They do not prove net profit or return on capital.
  • They do not prove order fillability or real execution speed.
  • They do not guarantee the same discrepancy will recur.
  • They do not prove current Paper or Real execution readiness.
