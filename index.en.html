ArbiFlow

From price discrepancies to economic arbitrage assessment

A financial-software engineering project focused on multi-market observation, arbitrage opportunity analysis, and a disciplined separation between an observed spread, an economically qualified opportunity, and an authorized action.

CASE STUDY · NOVEMBER 2026WALLEX / BITPIN / NOBITEX
01

What problem does it address?

A price discrepancy is the start of an analysis, not its financial conclusion.

In cross-exchange arbitrage, a visible buy/sell difference can look attractive while fees, accessible depth, order-size constraints, independently held venue inventory and data validity can make the trade economically unviable. ArbiFlow is being developed as a decision framework that keeps these layers distinct.
